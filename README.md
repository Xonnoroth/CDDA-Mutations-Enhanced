# CDDA Mutations Enhanced
Mutations Enhanced is a mod for Cataclysm: Dark Days Ahead (extremely WIP and depending on when I last bugfixed will probably just crash a world on load) that changes the timeline on the discovery of mutagenic compounds based on XE-037 (the blob) and the exact way they function in the lore. Mutations were significantly more developed in the world of Mutations Enhanced, leading to greater capabilities (both for monsters and the player), the potential for more targeted mutations, and physical mutations being generally less visually grotesque lore-wise compared to base DDA.

Mostly a personal mod and learning experience at the moment. If I actually get things into a decently working and satisfactory state I may try to get the mod in-repo.

# Cross Compatibility with other Mods
While the intent is to eventually have the mod be a complete and interesting addition to the game by itself, I intend to add various content that is exclusive to each one of the following mods:

- Magiclysm (Likely mana mutations to new categories I make and misc magical capabilities. I also have an idea for a Demon Spider mutation category, but that maybe could just go in Magiclysm itself.)
- Mind Over Matter (Base mod's lore has psychic and mutagenic research seperate, so there's lots of untapped potential for mutations and psychic power.)
- Xedra Evolved (Probably not much more than lore snippets that mesh the two togeteher. The mod already does a lot with the mutation system while mostly being seperate from mutagen so you can't be a Fae Vampire Mad Scientist, and I don't really disagree with that. Maybe give some of the supernatural creatures the odd extra mutation or buff if they are utterly outclassed by more traditional mutants, including fae enemies and such.)
- Arcana (Also probably lore snippets and the odd buff like XE. Most stuff I could come up with probably fits in Arcana itself. I highly doubt many eldritch god worshipping cultists are quite as concerned with not turning into a grotesque abomination.)
