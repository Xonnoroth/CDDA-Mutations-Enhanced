# CDDA Mutations Enhanced
Mutations Enhanced is a mod for Cataclysm: Dark Days Ahead (extremely WIP and currently doesn't do much other than not immediately crash on load) that changes the timeline on the discovery of mutagenic compounds based on XE-037 (the blob) and the exact way they function in the lore. Mutations were significantly more developed in the world of Mutations Enhanced, leading to greater capabilities (both for monsters and the player), the potential for more targeted mutations, and physical mutations being generally less visually grotesque lore-wise compared to the base game.

Mostly a personal mod and learning experience at the moment.
