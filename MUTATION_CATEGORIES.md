# M32 GEM (General Enhancement Module)
Prior to the Cataclysm, the M32 GEM was "standard issue" for any special operators that did not have the mental profile needed to deviate even slightly from the human form. While the strict cosmetic requirements necessary for the mental stability of such operators inhibit what is possible, the treatments still make someone verifiably superhuman in many aspects. A version of the Alpha mutation category that has been put through the R&D ringer, and largely enhanced with nearly all downsides removed, with a focus on making soldiers better at their jobs.

Primary benefits:
- All stats aside from intelligence gain a +12 (+4 for Int, but Fast Learner and Fast Reader). Number chosen to make even your average 8/8/8/8 human borderline superhuman, and a soldier undeniably so (especially given their largely unchanged physique).
- Toughened skin and body (more HP and a bit of natural armor that doesn't impede any clothing, but nothing that can completely stop even a .22 LR)
- Slightly increased speed and movement speed (Slightly relative to the rest of the mod, major relative to vanilla)
- Increased pain tolerance and healing capabilities
- Less sleep needed and slower to tire physically

Downsides/Mixed:
- Social penalties like Alpha that stem from superiority, but lessened
- Still having your insides become your outsides if a real mutant gets in melee with you

# XC-IS (Experimental Chimera - Infantry Support)
A decade-old failed experimental mutation category that sought to turn an elite soldier into a quadrupedal beast the size of a small pickup truck, in the hopes of filling a role similar to an IFV or tank. The program succeeded at doing exactly that, although at even greater cost than such vehicles, without 
addressing all the weaknesses of such vehicles, and adding weaknesses of their own (namely slower top speed and other soldiers being terrified of a tank-like beast that can and might eat them if rations ever run low).

Primary benefits:

- Absurd HP, armor, and regeneration (Nothing short of .50 BMG will do meaningful damage)
- Significantly increased overall speed
- When on all fours, movement cost reduction enough to move at up to 40 mph (with the flat speed mutation included)
- Virtually no stamina or weariness issues
- Boosts to all stats, extreme emphasis on strength (should be able to break down metal doors without a tool)
- Pair of tentacle-like limbs that function like medium machine guns and a tail that can launch projectiles akin to high-velocity grenades (preferably autonomously when active), only taking time and calories to refresh "ammo"

Downsides/Mixed:

- Absurd metabolism (much higher than vanilla Chimera)
- Huge size category (bigger, if we ever get one larger)
- Penalties for not being on all fours
- Unable to use firearms or operate vehicles, impossible or near impossible to craft
- Impossible/ineffective to use the majority of armor or clothing, natural armor should go all the way to the outer layer
