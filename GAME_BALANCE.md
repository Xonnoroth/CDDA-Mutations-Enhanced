# Overall
Mutations and mutant monsters will generally trend toward being stronger, but should still come with significant tradeoffs in exchange for that power (except for the improved version of Alpha, but that's intended to be weak compared to the other options). And now matter how many weaknesses are present, the player should never get to the point where they can tank an antimaterial rifle with their bare body completely unscathed (but not having a lethal wound can be fair game for some of them).

Ideally, a very capable baseline human should be able to still hypothetically survive the apocalypse as a whole, but it should be incredibly difficult, bordering on near impossible as evolution ramps up (highly evolved zombies are intended to be increased in lethality significantly). It should also require such an individual to carry a higher caliber weapon than a modern infantry rifle.

# Mutation Capabilities
In the mod, mutations are expected to be able to do highly complex things, even things that aren't found in nature and border on sci-fi nonsense. Short of near instantaneously becoming a 20-foot tall flesh mass (like a boss from later entries in the Resident Evil franchise), most things are fair game.

For example, complex chemical synthesis, organic supermaterials, and extremely targeted, rapid metabolizing are broadly what can be expected from highly mutated individuals. More specifically, more damage resistant upgrades of fur, scale, chitin, and flesh materials, powerful organic projectile/chemical launchers, and more impressively superhuman speed and long-term physical endurance (weariness) than base game mutations, are a few examples of what is not only fair game, but expected.
