# General
Mutations Enhanced tells of an Earth where portal technology led to the discovery of XE-037 and its uses decades prior to the founding of XEDRA and the major arms race in portal technology. This jumpstarted highly successful mutagenic programs that, within a few short years, moved from being an expensive, dangerous, and unethical way to create monsters, to being a way to make experimental supersoldiers. A few years after, mutants became an expensive (if rare) reality on the battlefield and in powerful individuals with strong government ties (Both in the U.S. and in any country with strong espionage capabilities). A few years prior to the Cataclysm, the process was considered safe and targeted enough to make a select handful of mutations available to the public for both personal enhancement (generally, nothing purely combat related) or cosmetics. Needless to say, the moment the public knew about any of this, it was an extreme political hot topic.

The spread of XE-037 was significantly less aggressive here initially for unknown reasons, as evidenced by the fact that technology derived from exploiting it didn't lead to mass zombification or the conditions needed for portal storms until years after it was an expensive but publicly available product.

# Base Game Mutation
Most of the mutation categories already in the game represent early mutagenic experimentation, with some of them being refined into something safer, more palatable, and sometimes less militarized, for an eccentric and wealthy audience. Generally speaking, most such mutation categories shouldn't see some of the advanced combat mutations that now exist, and probably shouldn't have much more changed than removing Ugly and it's mutation line, unless there's some notable capability the creature has that was too unreasonable to model in the base game.

The mutations from the base game themselves will generally be adjusted in flavor text, largely moving away from the grotesque, outside of some notable exceptions. It would be difficult to convince someone to willingly become a misshapen flesh sack, and the mutation process was still expensive and lengthy enough that forcing people the become biologically-enhanced "workers" or cannon fodder wasn't economically sound at any point prior to the Cataclysm. The body horror stuff is intended to still be present, but relegated to extremely unethical and definitely illegal experimental blacksites (the labs) that became more and more common as the portal technology arms race escalated.
